Why organisations trust us

Every candidate checked before they start

Our consultants work with dedicated compliance specialists, so the professionals we put forward meet the qualification, experience and safety requirements of each role.

Identity and right to work

Original documents checked to confirm identity and the right to work in the UK.

DBS checks

Criminal record checks at the level the role requires, including enhanced checks for work with children and vulnerable adults.

Registration and qualifications

Professional registration (such as the NMC register for nurses) and qualifications verified with the issuing body.

References and work history

Employment history reviewed and references taken, with any gaps explored.

Interview and suitability

Every candidate is interviewed so we understand their skills, experience and the settings they suit.

Matched to your setting

We recommend people for the needs of your service, not simply the first available name.

Checks follow the requirements of each role and setting, including NHS employment check standards and safer recruitment guidance for schools.

How it works

Simple steps, whichever side you’re on

For clients

  1. Tell us what you need. Send a staffing request.
  2. We confirm the details. We discuss and agree your requirements.
  3. We recommend candidates. You receive suitable, screened professionals.

For candidates

  1. Register or apply. Find a vacancy or register your interest with your CV.
  2. Speak to our team. We learn about your experience and goals.
  3. Registration and compliance. We guide you through the checks needed for each role.
  4. Get matched. We put you forward for suitable roles.

      Services/Education & SEN

      Education & SEN recruitment

      Teaching assistants, SEN support staff and one-to-one learning support for schools, special schools and alternative provision.

      Every pupil deserves support that fits them. We place teaching assistants and SEN professionals who can build trust with children and young people, follow their support plans and work closely with teachers and families.

      Request StaffSee education vacancies

      Roles we recruit for

      • SEN teaching assistants
      • One-to-one support assistants
      • Teaching assistants (primary and secondary)
      • Learning support assistants
      • Behaviour and pastoral support
      • Cover supervisors

      Settings we support

      • Mainstream primary and secondary schools
      • Special schools
      • Alternative provision and pupil referral units
      • SEN units and resource bases

      What clients can expect

      • Staff matched to pupils’ needs, including pupils with an education, health and care plan (EHCP).
      • Safer recruitment in line with the Department for Education’s Keeping children safe in education guidance.
      • Day-to-day, short-term and long-term support, including cover at short notice.
      • One point of contact who understands your school.

      For candidates: what you’ll usually need

      • An enhanced DBS check with the children’s barred list, or willingness to apply for one
      • The right to work in the UK
      • Experience with children or young people, ideally including special educational needs such as autism, ADHD, SEMH or speech and language needs
      • Two professional references

      Services/Nursery & early years

      Nursery & early years recruitment

      Qualified and experienced early years staff for nurseries and pre-schools, helping you stay within EYFS staff-to-child ratios.

      The early years shape everything that follows. We recruit warm, qualified practitioners who understand the Early Years Foundation Stage and can step into your rooms with confidence.

      Request StaffSee nursery and early years vacancies

      Roles we recruit for

      • Nursery assistants
      • Nursery practitioners (Level 2 and Level 3)
      • Early years educators
      • Room leaders
      • Early years SEN support
      • Deputy managers

      Settings we support

      • Day nurseries
      • Pre-schools
      • School nursery classes
      • Children’s centres

      What clients can expect

      • Qualifications checked against the Department for Education’s early years qualification requirements, so you know who counts in your ratios.
      • Staff who know the EYFS and your safeguarding and welfare duties.
      • Cover for absence, as well as fixed-term and permanent placements.
      • Paediatric first aid confirmed where your setting needs it.

      For candidates: what you’ll usually need

      • A full and relevant early years qualification (Level 2 or Level 3), or relevant experience
      • An enhanced DBS check, or willingness to apply for one
      • Paediatric first aid (desirable)
      • The right to work in the UK and two references

      Services/Healthcare

      Healthcare recruitment

      Nurses, healthcare assistants and support workers for NHS Trusts, Mental Health Trusts and independent providers, in ward-based and community services.

      Safe staffing protects patients and teams. We supply registered and unregistered healthcare professionals who are checked, prepared and ready to work in your service.

      Request StaffSee healthcare vacancies

      Roles we recruit for

      • Registered general nurses (RGN)
      • Registered mental health nurses (RMN)
      • Learning disability nurses (RNLD)
      • Healthcare assistants
      • Mental health support workers
      • Forensic and secure services support workers
      • Rehabilitation support workers

      Settings we support

      • Acute and ward-based services
      • Mental health services
      • Forensic and secure services
      • Rehabilitation
      • Community services
      • Substance misuse services

      What clients can expect

      • Pre-employment checks following the NHS Employers standards: identity, right to work, professional registration and qualifications, employment history and references, criminal record and work health assessment.
      • NMC registration verified for every nurse we supply.
      • Mandatory training checked before placement.
      • Temporary, fixed-term and permanent staffing.

      For candidates: what you’ll usually need

      • Current NMC registration for nursing roles
      • Up-to-date mandatory training, such as basic life support, moving and handling and safeguarding
      • An enhanced DBS check, or willingness to apply for one
      • The right to work in the UK and references covering recent employment

      Services/Social care

      Social care recruitment

      Support workers, carers and senior staff for residential and nursing homes, supported living, community services and registered children’s homes.

      Good care is built on people who care. We look for values as well as experience, so the staff we place treat every person with dignity, patience and respect.

      Request StaffSee social care vacancies

      Roles we recruit for

      • Support workers
      • Care assistants
      • Senior carers and team leaders
      • Dementia care staff
      • Residential childcare workers
      • Supported living staff

      Settings we support

      • Residential and nursing homes
      • Dementia care
      • Supported living and community services
      • Children’s and young people’s registered homes
      • Independent fostering agencies

      What clients can expect

      • Values-based recruitment: we look at attitude and approach as well as experience.
      • Training checked, including the Care Certificate or the newer Level 2 Adult Social Care Certificate where relevant.
      • Staff who understand the standards expected by the CQC in adult care and Ofsted in children’s homes.
      • Flexible cover for short notice, fixed-term and permanent needs.

      For candidates: what you’ll usually need

      • The Care Certificate or Level 2 Adult Social Care Certificate, or willingness to complete it
      • For children’s homes: the Level 3 Diploma in Residential Childcare, or willingness to work towards it
      • An enhanced DBS check, or willingness to apply for one
      • The right to work in the UK and two references
