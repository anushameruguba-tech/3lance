How it works

For candidates

  1. Register or apply. Find a vacancy or register your interest with your CV.
  2. Speak to our team. We learn about your experience and goals.
  3. Registration and compliance. We guide you through the checks needed for each role.
  4. Get matched. We put you forward for suitable roles.

For clients

  1. Tell us what you need. Send a staffing request.
  2. We confirm the details. We discuss and agree your requirements.
  3. We recommend candidates. You receive suitable, screened professionals.

About 3Lance

Placing the right people in the right roles, even at short notice.

At 3Lance, we have been providing high-quality staffing solutions to the health and social care sector for many years. We take pride in being recognised by our clients as a reputable, reliable and professional recruitment provider.

Our extensive experience and understanding of the sector enable us to place the right people in the right roles, even at short notice.
